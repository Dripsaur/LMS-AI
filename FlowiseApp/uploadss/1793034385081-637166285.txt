1.1 Presenting Problem and Likely Underlying Problems

Presenting problem

BIFC needs an independent Organizational Assessment and Improvement Plan before the first drawdown of its conditional ₱800-million loan. The assessment must examine organizational effectiveness and readiness for the technological transition associated with Project Talino. The report is due to the Board by 1 March 2027, before its 15 March 2027 meeting (case, pp. 2, 10–12). The bank’s Group 1 condition requires coverage of all five sites and interviews with at least 10% of rank-and-file employees. The COO’s refusal to permit Zamboanga access until April conflicts directly with the report deadline; it is a feasibility and acceptance risk, not a minor scheduling issue.

BIFC’s difficulties appear systemic. The hypotheses below must be tested by triangulating records, interviews, focus groups, and observation. A symptom does not by itself prove its cause.

Is Project Talino a cause, symptom, or trigger?

Project Talino is best treated as a trigger and possible contributing cause—not automatically the root cause. It has activated fears about job displacement, surveillance and broken promises, while the manner of communication and governance may add to mistrust. The assessment must separate verified decisions from rumors, assess both employees who fear the change and those who support it, and evaluate the human and organizational effects without selecting individual jobs or employees for elimination.

1.3 Primary Client, Signatory and Relevant Client System

Primary client and contract signatory

We recommend BIFC as the primary client and the CEO as the proposed management signatory, only after corporate authority is confirmed and the Board approves or acknowledges governance as required. This is preferable to naming Jerome alone: he initiated the engagement but cannot represent every site, the workforce or the lender’s scope condition. It is also preferable to treating the CEO as the personal client, because she must not control the consultants’ conclusions or obtain identifiable employee responses. The bank’s requirements should be documented as acceptance criteria, but the bank should not direct interviews or dictate findings.

Relevant client system

Diagnose BIFC as a whole, including all five sites and the Villaflor family-business leadership system only where family relationships affect corporate authority, communication, decision-making or organizational functioning. Excluding Zamboanga would omit an acquired plant with its own workforce and union, integration challenges and local context. Private family matters unrelated to BIFC’s work remain outside scope.

Group 1 twist: Zamboanga access conflict

The COO’s ‘off-limits until April’ instruction cannot be reconciled with a 1 March 2027 report deadline if the bank requires all five sites. Before full commitment, request written site access within the engagement window and explain the purpose, privacy safeguards, local-language support and production-disruption controls. Escalate through the agreed sponsor/Board governance route and ask the bank to confirm in writing whether any remote or independent local-partner method would satisfy its condition. Remote interviews and records may supplement, but must not be represented as equivalent to full site access without bank approval. If access cannot be secured and the bank does not formally revise its requirement or deadline, pause or decline the original scope rather than claim compliance falsely.

1.4 BOEP Fit, Stakeholder Agendas and Go/No-Go Decision

Consulting-firm fit and safeguards

BOEP is a proposed local OD firm for this classroom case. Its fit must be demonstrated rather than assumed: the engagement needs organizational diagnosis, labor-relations sensitivity, privacy-safe research, manufacturing/ERP understanding and culturally appropriate multilingual fieldwork across five sites. The case does not establish that BOEP itself has all six working languages or specialist capacity. Therefore, before contracting, verify relevant credentials and capacity, disclose any limitations, and engage qualified independent local-language partners—especially for Zamboanga—under the same confidentiality rules. Do not promise language coverage or access that has not been secured.

The engagement lead’s relationship as the HR Director’s former classmate and kumpare must be disclosed in writing to BIFC’s authorized governance body and the lender where appropriate. BOEP should appoint an independent lead reviewer, exclude the conflicted lead from sole control of evidence interpretation, and document conflict-management steps. If independence cannot be credibly protected, BOEP should replace the lead or decline.

Decision: CONDITIONAL GO

BIFC has a credible need for independent diagnosis, but BOEP should proceed only when all conditions below are met in writing:

All-five-site coverage, including Zamboanga, is authorized within the engagement window—or the bank formally accepts a documented alternative or revised deadline.

HR provides a verified rank-and-file roster and denominator. The interview target is at least 10% of rank-and-file employees, not automatically 10% of all 1,140 staff. If the rank-and-file denominator is not yet known, 114 is only a provisional planning floor (10% of total workforce), not proof of compliance; recalculate once verified.

BIFC appoints an authorized sponsor/signatory and a neutral liaison; the steering arrangement includes appropriate management, employee-representation and privacy perspectives without giving any stakeholder control over findings.

A signed agreement protects voluntary participation, confidentiality, consultant independence, no retaliation, data minimization, retention/destruction and restrictions on AI use. CEO access to names/transcripts and the ID-linked portal are not accepted.

The kumpare relationship is disclosed and managed; qualified language support and independent review are secured.

Scope, timeline, budget, access to records/people, union engagement and decision rights are realistic and agreed. The diagnosis is not a disguised process for choosing employees or jobs for displacement.

If any essential condition fails, do not proceed under the original scope. Pause while negotiating a written scope/deadline change, partner with an independent qualified firm if that resolves capacity or conflict, or decline. The all-five-site and 10% interview conditions must not be described as satisfied without evidence.

Conclusion

BIFC’s presenting problem is the lender-required independent assessment; likely underlying problems include weakened performance, declining trust, leadership and decision-rights conflict, labor-relations concerns, inconsistent systems adoption, post-acquisition integration and inadequate AI governance. Weisbord’s Six-Box, Burke–Litwin and Nadler–Tushman provide complementary ways to test these hypotheses. The recommended decision is a conditional GO, with the Zamboanga access conflict and consultant independence resolved before full commitment.
